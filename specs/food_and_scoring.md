# Food And Scoring

## Food

Place one food randomly on the map. It must not spawn on the snake’s body.

Even if the body eventually moves away, the player has no control over when that happens. The food could be trapped behind the snake’s own body, forcing the player to wait or take risky moves. That feels unfair and breaks the flow.

## Growth and score

Eating food increases the snake’s length by 1 and the score by 1. Then place new food randomly.
